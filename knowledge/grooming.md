# Grooming and hygiene

## Brushing
Brush in the direction the fur grows, with the brush the owner provides. Short, calm sessions are better than long ones. Stop if the pet becomes stressed.

## Litter boxes
Scoop litter boxes at least once a day. Cats may stop using a dirty box. A cat straining in the litter box without producing urine is an emergency, especially in male cats.

## Ears, eyes, and paws
Check for redness, discharge, or limping during grooming and tell the owner about anything unusual. Do not trim nails unless the owner asked you to.
