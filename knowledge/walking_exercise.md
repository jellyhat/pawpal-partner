# Walks and exercise

## Walk routine
Keep walks at roughly the same time and length as the owner's routine. Dogs often expect to go out first thing in the morning and again before bed.

## Leash and safety
Always use the leash and harness the owner provides. Check that the collar or harness is snug enough that the dog cannot back out of it. Keep the dog away from unfamiliar dogs unless the owner says it is fine.

## Hot and cold weather
On hot days walk early or late and avoid hot pavement; if the pavement is too hot for the back of your hand, it is too hot for paws. Watch for heavy panting or slowing down. In cold weather, keep walks shorter for small or short-haired dogs.

## When a walk has to move
If you cannot walk the dog in its usual window, a short bathroom break at the usual time followed by the full walk later is better than skipping it.
