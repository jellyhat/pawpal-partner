# Foods and items that are dangerous to pets

## Dogs
Chocolate, grapes and raisins, xylitol (a sweetener found in sugar-free gum and some peanut butters), onions, garlic, macadamia nuts, alcohol, and cooked bones can all be dangerous to dogs. Keep trash and countertops clear.

## Cats
Lilies (every part of the plant, even pollen or vase water) are extremely toxic to cats. Onions, garlic, chocolate, and grapes are also dangerous. Keep string, hair ties, and rubber bands out of reach because cats may swallow them.

## If a pet eats something toxic
Call the vet or an emergency animal hospital right away, even if the pet seems fine. Note what was eaten, about how much, and when. Do not try to make the pet vomit unless a vet tells you to.
