# Giving medication safely

## Follow instructions exactly
Give only the medication, dose, and timing the owner wrote down. Never change the dose, skip a dose, or give an extra dose unless the vet tells you to.

## Missed doses
If you notice a dose was missed, do not give a double dose. Contact the owner or the vet and ask what to do.

## Hiding pills
Many pets accept pills wrapped in a pill pocket, a small piece of cheese, or wet food, if the owner says that food is allowed. Watch to make sure the pill is swallowed and not spat out later.

## Human medicine
Never give a pet human pain relievers or cold medicine. Ibuprofen, acetaminophen, naproxen, and aspirin can be toxic to dogs and cats.
