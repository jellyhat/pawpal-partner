# Emergencies and when to call the vet

## Call a vet immediately for
Trouble breathing, choking, seizures, collapse, heavy bleeding, a swollen or hard belly with retching (possible bloat in dogs), eating something toxic, being hit by a car, or a cat straining to urinate with little or nothing coming out.

## What to have ready
Keep the owner's vet phone number and the nearest 24-hour emergency animal hospital handy. Know where the carrier and leash are.

## Contact the owner
After the pet is safe, contact the owner and tell them what happened and what the vet said.
