# Feeding basics

## Sticking to the owner's portions
Feed exactly the amount and food the owner wrote down. Sudden changes in food type or amount are a common cause of upset stomachs in dogs and cats. Do not switch brands or add new treats while the owner is away.

## Late or missed meals
If a meal is a little late, feed the normal portion when you can. Do not double the next meal to make up for a missed one; just return to the normal schedule.

## Water
Pets should always have fresh, clean water. Check and refill the bowl at every visit or feeding, and wash it daily.

## Appetite changes
A pet skipping a single meal can be normal, especially a cat stressed by the owner being away. A pet that refuses food for more than a day, or is also vomiting or very tired, should be reported to the owner and the vet.
